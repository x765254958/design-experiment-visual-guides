# Example: two-strain CFU screening

Use this only as a structural example and only in an approved microbiology laboratory.

## Question

At matched total-particle concentrations, do blank carrier and loaded particles change recoverable colony-forming units after direct contact?

## Minimum logic

- medium blank;
- bacterial growth control;
- laboratory-validated positive control;
- blank carrier at selected concentrations;
- loaded particles at matched total-particle concentrations;
- no-bacteria material backgrounds;
- separate execution for each strain.

## Day structure

- pre-day: confirm strain identity, prepare labels and media, revive culture according to the laboratory SOP.
- D0: prepare inoculum, verify starting level, prepare material working solutions, set up contact assay.
- D1: mix consistently, perform serial dilutions, plate selected dilutions.
- D2: photograph plates, count colonies, calculate CFU, apply quality controls.

## Visuals

- inoculum and material preparation;
- plate/contact layout;
- serial-dilution ladder;
- spread-plating workflow;
- countability and CFU calculation.

## Boundary

CFU reduction after a fixed contact period is not automatically MIC or MBC. When active loading is unknown, it also does not establish an active-drug-equivalent comparison.

# Output contract

## Full-pack directory

```text
project-name/
├── experiment_spec.json
├── protocol.docx
├── beginner-manual.docx
├── source-matrix.md
├── visual-prompts/
│   ├── D0-layout.txt
│   ├── D0-operation.txt
│   └── ...
├── figures/
│   ├── D0-layout.png
│   ├── D0-operation.png
│   └── ...
└── raw-records/
```

## Written plan order

1. Objective and claim boundary.
2. Known conditions and unresolved items.
3. Groups and dose basis.
4. Experimental sequence and dependencies.
5. Methods matched one-to-one with objectives.
6. Materials, instruments, and outsourced tests.
7. Quality controls and stop criteria.
8. Expected outputs/figures, not fabricated results.
9. Raw-data and naming plan.
10. Safety and approval notes.

## Beginner manual style

- Use direct laboratory language.
- Keep headings as actual experiment/action names.
- Explain why a step exists only where it prevents a common error.
- Put calculations in tables with one worked example.
- Mark decisions that require local confirmation.
- Avoid generic introductions, exaggerated claims, and AI-like repetition.

## Delivery QA

- Render DOCX/PDF/slides and inspect every page.
- Inspect generated images at original resolution.
- Confirm filenames and cross-links.
- Preserve editable source files where possible.
